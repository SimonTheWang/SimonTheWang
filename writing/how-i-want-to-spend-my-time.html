How I want to spend my time.

September 2026

It was simpler when I first was working in entrepreneurship. I simply needed to dedicate my every moment into working.

I’m not sure when, but along the line, I started looking at what else had to offer. For some time lately, this felt wrong, as many entrepreneurs I respect have long claimed that all they do is work.

While it feels odd, it doesn’t feel wrong to want to expand as a person beyond my work. At times, I wonder if it is simply because I don’t believe my work is fulfilling enough. While there may be a dozen reasons why I feel this way. I do believe the only valid path is forward.

I therefore give myself full permission to explore, to enjoy, and to reap the benefits of what I want to consider a full life.
